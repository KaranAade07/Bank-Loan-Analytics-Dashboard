# Bank-Loan-Analytics-Dashboard
Interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze loan applications, customer distribution, loan status, and loan amount across different cities and loan types.


## 📌 Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard analyzes bank loan data to monitor loan approvals, customer distribution, loan types, and city-wise loan performance. It helps management understand lending patterns and customer trends.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The bank management wants to:

- Track total loan amount
- Monitor approved, pending, and rejected loans
- Identify high-value customers
- Compare loan types
- Analyze city-wise loan distribution

---

## 📊 KPIs

- Total Loan Amount
- Approved Loan Amount
- Total Customers
- Approval Rate

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- Loan Amount by Loan Type
- Loan Status Distribution
- Top Customers by Loan Amount
- Loan Amount by City
- Interactive Filters
- Customer Details Table

---

## 💡 Business Insights

- Home Loans contribute the highest loan amount.
- Most applications are approved.
- Delhi has the highest loan value.
- Home Loan customers have higher average loan amounts than Personal Loan customers.

---
